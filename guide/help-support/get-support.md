# Get Support

If you have already gone through the **Docs** and the [FAQ](/reference/faq) and you are still running into a problem with **Fluent Members**, our support team is ready to help.

## How to Contact Support

To get the fastest and most accurate response, please follow the steps below when you reach out to us:

1. **Visit the WPManageNinja Support Portal:** Head over to [WPManageNinja](https://wpmanageninja.com/account). This is the main channel for opening Fluent Members support tickets.

2. **Submit a Support Ticket:**
   - Sign in with your account (the same account you use on [WPManageNinja.com](https://wpmanageninja.com/account)).
   - Open the **Support Tickets** section.
   - Click **Create Ticket**.

3. **Provide Detailed Information:** When you open the ticket, share as much context as you can. The more we know, the faster we can pinpoint the cause and send a fix. Please include:

   - **A clear description of the problem:** Tell us what you are trying to do and what is going wrong instead. For example: *"I set up a Membership Level with Stripe, but after checkout the member's status stays Pending"* or *"The Login Popup does not appear for restricted posts."*
   - **Steps to reproduce:** Walk us through the exact sequence of clicks or actions that triggers the issue.
   - **Screenshots or screen recordings:** Screenshots of your Level settings, Access Group configuration, the member's detail page, and any visible error on the frontend help us enormously.
   - **Error messages:** If you see anything in a red box, a browser console error, or a line in your WordPress debug log, copy it into the ticket as text.
   - **Your WordPress version.**
   - **Your Fluent Members Free version** — and, if applicable, **your Fluent Members Pro version.**
   - **Your PHP version** (found under **Tools → Site Health → Info → Server**).
   - **Any other active plugins on your site:** List them, especially anything related to email delivery (FluentSMTP, WP Mail SMTP), caching (WP Rocket, LiteSpeed, W3 Total Cache), security (Wordfence, iThemes Security), payments (WooCommerce, FluentCart, Paymattic), or page builders (Elementor, Bricks, Gutenberg blocks). These are the most common sources of conflict for membership access, checkout flows, and email notifications.

4. **Support Hours:** Our team works during regular business hours and aims to respond to every ticket as quickly as possible.

We are committed to helping you get the most out of Fluent Members!
