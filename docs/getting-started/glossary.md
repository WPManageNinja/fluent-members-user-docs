# Glossary

This **glossary** explains the terms used across the Fluent Members docs. Use it when a word in a guide or on a screen isn't clear.

## A

**Active:** The normal membership status. The member has access to everything their level unlocks.

**Administrator:** WordPress administrators skip all protection, so they always see everything. Test your protection in a private window while logged out. See [How protection works](/docs/content-access/how-protection-works).

**Admin bar badge (Pro):** The **Test Mode** or **Live Mode** badge administrators see in the WordPress admin bar. See [Test mode and live mode](/docs/getting-started/test-and-live-mode).

## B

**Billing (Pro):** The top bar menu that holds **Subscriptions** and **One-time**. See [Billing overview](/docs/billing/).

**Block email editor (Pro):** An editor for designing email notifications with blocks, starting from a template if you want. See [Custom email editor](/docs/settings/email-configuration/custom-email-editor).

**Bundle:** A named set of content that more than one Membership Level can unlock. You create one from a level's **Content Access** tab with **Save as a bundle**, then edit it in a drawer. Formerly called an access group. See [Bundles](/docs/content-access/bundles).

## C

**Cancellation mode (Pro):** Decides when a cancelled subscription ends. **End of Billing Period** keeps access until the paid time runs out. **Cancel Immediately** ends it right away. See [Cancel a subscription](/docs/billing/cancelling-a-subscription).

**Cancelled:** A membership status for a membership someone has ended.

**Checkout:** The page where a visitor pays. It belongs to the plugin that sells the level, or to Fluent Members itself when you use Native Payment (Pro). See [Checkout & guest checkout](/docs/selling/checkout).

**Content Access:** The tab on a Membership Level where you choose what the level unlocks: its own content, other memberships it includes, Bundles, and FluentCommunity spaces and courses. See [Content access](/docs/levels/content-access).

**Corporate Child (Pro):** The label on a member who holds a seat on someone else's team membership. Their seat ends when the owner's membership ends. See [Corporate members](/docs/members/corporate-members).

**Currency:** The default currency for new prices, set under **Settings → General Settings**. Existing prices, subscriptions and payments keep their own currency. See [General settings](/docs/settings/general).

## D

**Dashboard:** The first screen of the plugin. Its cards show **Active Members**, **Revenue**, **Recurring Revenue** and **Member Churn**, above charts, the Launch checklist and recent activity. See [Read your dashboard](/docs/dashboard/dashboard).

**Drip content:** Releases protected content over time instead of all at once. See **Release schedule**.

## E

**Email notification:** An automatic email for a membership event, such as **Welcome Email**, **Payment Receipt** or **Membership Expiring**. See [Email notifications](/docs/settings/email-configuration/email-notifications).

**Entire Website:** A rule type that protects every page on your site. See [Protect content by type](/docs/content-access/protected-content).

**Expired:** A membership status set when a membership's end date passes. The member loses access and the record stays.

## F

**FluentCart:** An e-commerce plugin. Sell a level as a FluentCart product, and the membership is granted when the order is paid. See [FluentCart](/docs/integrations/fluentcart).

**FluentCommunity:** A community plugin. Give members spaces and courses automatically when they join a level. See [FluentCommunity](/docs/integrations/fluent-community).

**FluentCRM:** A CRM plugin. Start automations when a membership changes, and sync levels with tags. See [FluentCRM](/docs/integrations/fluentcrm).

**Fluent Forms:** A form plugin. Sell a level through a payment form. See [Fluent Forms](/docs/integrations/fluent-forms).

**Fluent Members Pro:** The paid plugin that adds to the free one. See [Free vs Pro](/docs/getting-started/free-vs-pro).

**Fluent Support:** A helpdesk plugin. Agents see a customer's memberships inside their ticket. See [Fluent Support](/docs/integrations/fluent-support).

**Free level:** A level anyone can join without paying. See [Free levels](/docs/levels/free-levels).

## G

**Guest checkout (Pro):** Buying a native plan without being logged in. The visitor enters an email address at checkout and the account is created for them. See [Checkout & guest checkout](/docs/selling/checkout).

**Guided setup:** The five steps that open the first time you open the plugin on a new install: welcome, get paid, your first membership, content and live. See [Guided setup & quick start](/docs/getting-started/quick-start).

## H

**Hook:** An extension point for developers. Actions run code on an event, and filters change a value.

## I

**Integration:** A connection to another plugin. See [Integrations overview](/docs/integrations/).

**Invitation (Pro):** How a team owner fills a seat. The person gets an emailed link and joins the team. See [Corporate seat invites](/docs/members/portal/corporate-seat-invites).

## L

**Launch checklist:** A card on the Dashboard, and a panel on the last setup step, that lists what is left before you go live. Each item ticks itself when your data shows it is done. See [Guided setup & quick start](/docs/getting-started/quick-start).

**Leave out:** An option on a Content Access rule that takes some items out of that content only. See [Content access](/docs/levels/content-access).

**Level automation:** A FluentCRM automation tied to a level, listed on the level's **Automations** tab. See [Level automations](/docs/levels/automations).

**Level integration:** An action in another plugin that runs when a member joins or leaves a level, set on the level's **Integrations** tab. See [Level integrations](/docs/levels/integrations).

**Live mode:** The **Site Mode** setting that takes real payments through Stripe and PayPal. See **Site Mode**.

## M

**Member:** A WordPress user who holds at least one membership. A member can hold several at once. See [Members list](/docs/members/).

**Member Portal:** The page where logged-in members see and manage their memberships, using the `[fluent_member_portal]` shortcode. See [Set up the portal](/docs/members/portal/setup).

**Membership:** The record linking one WordPress user to one level, with its status, dates and provider. See [Member details](/docs/members/detail).

**Membership Level:** The plan a member buys or is given. It has a pricing type and unlocks content through its **Content Access** tab. See [Levels overview](/docs/levels/).

**Membership status:** The current state of a membership: **Active**, **Trial**, **Suspended**, **Cancelled**, **Expired** or **Upgraded**. Only **Active** and **Trial** give access.

**Migration:** Importing levels, members and more from Paid Memberships Pro, MemberPress or Kadence Memberships. See [Migration overview](/docs/settings/migration/).

## N

**Native Payment (Pro):** The built-in Stripe and PayPal checkout, as opposed to selling through another plugin. The guided setup calls it **Built-in checkout**. See [Price with native payments](/docs/levels/pricing-native).

## O

**One-time membership (Pro):** A membership with no recurring subscription behind it, listed under **Billing → One-time**. See [One-time memberships](/docs/billing/one-time).

## P

**Paymattic:** A payment form plugin. Sell a level through a Paymattic form. See [Paymattic](/docs/integrations/paymattic).

**Payment method (Pro):** The saved card on a Stripe subscription. Members can replace it from the portal. PayPal subscriptions do not offer this. See [Update payment method](/docs/members/portal/updating-payment-method).

**Paywall:** A link between a level and a product or form in another plugin. When the purchase completes, the member gets the level. See [Price with paywalls](/docs/levels/pricing-paywalls).

**Permissions:** A setting that decides who can add, change and remove access restrictions on posts, pages and blocks, with extra permissions for named **Managers**. See [Permissions](/docs/settings/permissions).

**Pricing plan (Pro):** One way to pay for a level with Native Payment, such as monthly or yearly. See [Price with native payments](/docs/levels/pricing-native).

**Pricing Type:** How a level is paid for: FluentCart, Fluent Forms, Paymattic, WooCommerce (Pro), Native Payment (Pro) or Free. See [Create a level](/docs/levels/creating).

**Prorate (Pro):** An option on an upgrade path that credits what a member has already paid toward the new plan. See [Upgrade paths](/docs/levels/upgrade-paths).

**Provider:** The system that created a membership, such as a paywall plugin, a gateway, a migration or a manual assignment. See [Member details](/docs/members/detail).

**Public content:** Pages and posts everyone can see, even when a membership covers them. Formerly called always-open content. See [Public content](/docs/content-access/public-content).

## R

**Recent Activity:** A panel on the dashboard that lists the latest membership events, filtered by **All**, **Members** or **Admin**. See [Read your dashboard](/docs/dashboard/dashboard).

**Recurring billing:** Charging a member automatically on a schedule. Use FluentCart subscriptions, or Stripe, PayPal or WooCommerce Subscriptions with Pro. See [Subscriptions](/docs/billing/subscriptions).

**Redirect:** A **Non-members see** choice that sends visitors without access to another page. See [Restricted content](/docs/content-access/restricted-content).

**Release schedule:** The setting on a Content Access rule that decides when members get it: immediately, after an amount of time since they join, or on a date. Content that has not opened yet shows **Unlocking soon** in the portal. See [Release schedule](/docs/content-access/content-drip).

**Restricted content:** The settings for what visitors without access see by default: a teaser, a message or a redirect. Each Bundle can customize its own. See [Restricted content](/docs/content-access/restricted-content).

**Restricted Content block:** A block that protects whatever you place inside it, instead of the whole post. See [Restricted Content block](/docs/content-access/gutenberg-block/inserting).

## S

**Seat (Pro):** One place on a team membership. **Maximum Member** sets how many there are, and the owner counts as one. See [Corporate memberships](/docs/levels/corporate-memberships).

**Shortcode:** A tag you paste into a page: `[fluent_membership_level]`, `[fluent_membership_levels]`, `[fluent_members]` or `[fluent_member_portal]`.

**Site Mode (Pro):** The switch under **Settings → General Settings** that puts Stripe and PayPal payments in **Test** or **Live** mode for the whole site. See [Test mode and live mode](/docs/getting-started/test-and-live-mode).

**Smartcode:** A placeholder in an email, such as <span v-pre>`{{user_name}}`</span> or <span v-pre>`{{membership_level}}`</span>, replaced with real data when the email is sent.

**Stripe (Pro):** A payment gateway for Native Payment. See [Set up Stripe](/docs/settings/payment-settings/stripe-setup).

**Subscription (Pro):** A recurring payment behind a membership, listed under **Billing → Subscriptions**. See [Subscriptions](/docs/billing/subscriptions).

**Suspended:** A membership status that removes access until it is restored. See [Suspend or cancel a member](/docs/members/suspending-and-cancelling).

## T

**Team membership (Pro):** A level for teams. One owner holds the membership and invites people to its seats. Also called a corporate membership. See [Corporate memberships](/docs/levels/corporate-memberships).

**Team owner (Pro):** The person who holds a team membership and manages its seats from the portal. People in those seats show as **Corporate Child** on their member details. See [Corporate members](/docs/members/corporate-members).

**Teaser:** A **Non-members see** choice that shows visitors the start of a protected post, followed by a message and a button. See [Restricted content](/docs/content-access/restricted-content).

**Test mode:** The **Site Mode** setting that runs Stripe and PayPal with test or sandbox credentials, so no real money is charged. It is the default. See [Test mode and live mode](/docs/getting-started/test-and-live-mode).

**Transaction (Pro):** One payment event, such as a charge, renewal or refund, listed under **Transaction History** on a subscription or one-time membership. See [Billing overview](/docs/billing/).

**Trial:** A membership status during a free trial. Trial members have access.

**Trial Days (Pro):** The free days at the start of a recurring pricing plan. See [Price with native payments](/docs/levels/pricing-native).

## U

**Upgrade path (Pro):** A move a member can make from one pricing plan to another, with an optional discount or proration. See [Upgrade paths](/docs/levels/upgrade-paths).

**Upgraded:** The status left on a membership after the member moved to another level or plan.

## W

**Webhook:** A message Stripe or PayPal sends to your site about a payment event, which keeps membership statuses up to date. See [Set up Stripe](/docs/settings/payment-settings/stripe-setup) or [Set up PayPal](/docs/settings/payment-settings/paypal-setup).

**WooCommerce (Pro):** An e-commerce plugin. Sell a level as a product, and restrict products to members. See [WooCommerce](/docs/integrations/woocommerce).
