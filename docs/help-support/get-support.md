# Get Support

Open a support ticket when [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/docs/help-support/faq) don't solve your problem with **Fluent Members**.

## Open a Ticket

1. Go to the [WPManageNinja account page](https://wpmanageninja.com/account) and sign in.
2. Open the **Support Tickets** section.
3. Click **Create Ticket**.

## What to Include

The more context your ticket has, the faster the team can find the cause:

- **The problem:** what you are trying to do and what happens instead. For example, "After checkout, the member has no membership" or "The login popup doesn't appear on restricted posts."
- **Steps to reproduce:** the exact clicks or actions that trigger the problem.
- **Screenshots or recordings:** your level settings, Access Group, the member's details page and any error on the screen.
- **Error messages:** any text from a red notice, the browser console or your WordPress debug log, pasted as text.
- **Versions:** WordPress, PHP, Fluent Members and, if you use it, Fluent Members Pro. You can find these under **Tools → Site Health → Info**.
- **Other active plugins:** list them all, especially email delivery, caching, security, payment and page builder plugins. These are the most common sources of conflict.
