# Corporate Memberships (Pro)

A **Corporate Membership** (Pro) gives one buyer, the **Team owner**, a set of seats to share. The owner invites colleagues from the Member Portal, and each person gets the level's access through their own WordPress account. Use it for agencies, companies and other teams.

In the admin, a corporate level is called a **Team** level when you create it.

## Create a team level

1. Go to **Fluent Members → Membership Levels** and click **New membership**.
2. Enter a **Name**, such as `Agency Plan`.
3. Under **How do people get it?**, choose **Team** ("One buyer, many seats, email invites").
4. Enter the seat limit in **Seats per team**. The owner counts as one seat, so the minimum is 2. Leave it empty for unlimited seats.
5. Choose how the level is sold and add a first price, as for any [paid level](/docs/levels/creating).
6. Click **Create membership**.

![Screenshot of the New membership dialog with Team selected and Seats per team set to 10](/images/levels/corporate-memberships/corporate-memberships-1.webp)

> [!Note]
> The **Team** card shows a **Pro** badge and can't be selected without Fluent Members Pro. A team level can't be a free level.

To change the seat limit later, edit **Maximum Members** on the level's **Setup** tab and click **Save**. In the levels list, a team level shows **Corporate · up to 10 seats** under its name and the seat limit under its member count.

## Add pricing and content

A team level needs the same setup as any other level:

1. On the **Pricing** tab, add a [Native Payment plan](/docs/levels/pricing-native) or a [paywall](/docs/levels/pricing-paywalls). The owner pays this price for the team.
2. On the **Content Access** tab, [choose what the level unlocks](/docs/levels/content-access). Everyone on the team gets the same access.

You can also make someone a Team owner yourself with [Assign Members](/docs/levels/members-on-a-level#assign-members-to-a-level). Each person you select becomes a separate owner with their own seats, not a seat on an existing team. The dialog warns you about this.

## How the owner manages the team

After buying the level, the owner opens the **Member Portal** and finds the **Team Members** panel on their membership. The panel shows the seat count, such as `3 / 10 seats`, and any pending invitations.

To invite someone, the owner enters their email under **Invite Member** and clicks **Send Invitation**. An invitation lasts 3 days by default. The invitee opens the link. If they already have an account, they log in and click **Accept Invitation**. If they don't, they can fill in their name and a password and click **Create Account and Join**.

From a row's menu in the panel, the owner can:

- **View invitation**, **Resend invitation** or **Cancel invitation** while it's pending.
- Cancel a team member to free their seat.

A team member's seat ends when the owner's membership ends. When a team owner moves to a smaller plan, they may need to remove members first. See [Upgrade Paths](/docs/levels/upgrade-paths) and [Corporate Seat Invites](/docs/members/portal/corporate-seat-invites) for the full flow.
