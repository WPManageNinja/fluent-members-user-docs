# What You Can Protect

Every level and [Bundle](/docs/content-access/bundles) protects content through the **Add content** dialog. You can protect a whole group of content, so new items are covered as you publish them, or pick single items.

## Open the dialog

1. Go to **Fluent Members → Membership Levels**, open a level and click the **Content Access** tab.
2. In the **Content** card, click **Add content**.
3. Pick a type tab, tick what to protect, and click **Add (n) items**.
4. Click **Save** in the save bar.

![Screenshot of the Add content dialog on the Everything tab with the type tabs and the Whole groups list](/images/content-access/protected-content/add-content-dialog-everything.webp)

The tabs along the top filter the list: **Everything**, **Pages**, **Posts**, **Categories & tags**, then **FluentCart** and **WooCommerce** products, and your custom post types. A search box finds single items by title.

## Whole groups

Whole groups protect everything of a kind, including content you publish later.

| Option | What it protects |
|---|---|
| **Entire website** | Every page of your site, including archives, the blog page and feeds. Public content and system pages stay open. |
| **All posts** | Every post. |
| **All pages** | Every page. |
| **All category archives** | Every post, the same as **All posts**. |
| **All tag archives** | Every post, the same as **All posts**. |
| **All (post type)** | Every item of a custom post type, such as **All Courses**. |

> [!Note]
> **Entire website** is the broadest rule. Your pricing, checkout and member portal pages are system pages, so they stay public. Add any other page visitors need to reach to [Public content](/docs/content-access/public-content).

### FluentCart products

These appear when FluentCart is active:

| Option | What it protects |
|---|---|
| **All Products** | Every FluentCart product. |
| **All Digital Products** | Every digital product. |
| **All Physical Products** | Every physical product. |
| **All Product Categories** | Every product, the same as **All Products**. |
| **All Product Brands** | Every product, the same as **All Products**. |

### WooCommerce products (Pro)

These need Fluent Members Pro and WooCommerce. Without Pro, the **WooCommerce** tab shows a **Pro** badge.

| Option | What it protects |
|---|---|
| **WC All Products** | Every WooCommerce product. |
| **WC All Product Categories Archive** | Every product, the same as **WC All Products**. |
| **WC All Product Tags Archive** | Every product, the same as **WC All Products**. |
| **WC All Product Brands Archive** | Every product, the same as **WC All Products**. |
| **WC All Product Shipping Classes Archive** | Every product, the same as **WC All Products**. |

## Single items

Under **Single items**, search and tick the pages, posts, categories, tags, products or custom post type items you want. Picking a category or tag protects the posts assigned to it, including posts you add later. Terms from custom taxonomies can be picked too.

![Screenshot of the Add content dialog with a search open and one result ticked](/images/content-access/protected-content/add-content-dialog-search.webp)

A badge on a result tells you more:

- **Public now:** Open to everyone today. Adding it makes it members only.
- **Also in (level):** Another membership unlocks it too.
- **Already added** and **Already in (level) via (membership):** You can't add it twice.

## Leave items out

To protect a broad group except for a few items, add the group, then use **Leave out content** in the three-dot menu. See [Leave out content](/docs/levels/content-access). A left-out item is skipped for this level's own content only. If another membership or Bundle covers it, that membership's members still need to hold it.

To make an item open for everyone instead, add it to [Public content](/docs/content-access/public-content).

## Test your rules

Administrators skip all protection, so test in a private window or with a member account. [How protection works](/docs/content-access/how-protection-works) explains which check decides the result.
